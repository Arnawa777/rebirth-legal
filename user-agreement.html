Rebirth Health User Agreement

Effective date: 20 August 2026

This User Agreement governs your use of Rebirth Health (Project Rebirth), a local-first Android wellness and personal-progression application. By installing or using the app, you agree to use it responsibly and consistently with this agreement.

1. Intended use

The app helps you record personal measurements, set journey goals and milestones, manage reminders, and review personal progress. It is intended for individual wellness and personal organization, not for diagnosis, treatment, emergency response, or clinical decision-making.

2. Your data and choices

You choose what information to enter and whether to enable optional HUAWEI Health or direct Huawei Watch GT 4 features. Manual entry remains available when a wearable, Huawei service, Bluetooth connection, permission, or network is unavailable.

3. Optional wearable integrations

HUAWEI Health access is read-only in the current alpha and is subject to compatible devices, HMS Core, HUAWEI Health, authorization, regional availability, and Huawei service terms. The direct Huawei Watch GT 4 option is experimental: the current slice supports discovery and operating-system pairing, but does not yet promise health-record synchronization. Wearable values may be delayed, incomplete, unavailable, or different from manual measurements.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Use the app only for lawful personal purposes.
  • Review entries and wearable values before relying on them for personal decisions.
  • Keep your Android device, accounts, permissions, and exported backup files secure.
  • Do not use the app to monitor another person without their knowledge and authorization.
  • Do not attempt to interfere with, reverse engineer, or misuse connected services or devices.

5. Backups and local storage

The app is designed to work locally on your device. You are responsible for deciding where exported backup files are stored and for keeping copies you need. A backup may contain personal information and should be handled as private.

6. Third-party services

When you enable Huawei features, your use is also governed by the applicable Huawei, HMS Core, HUAWEI Health, Android, device, and distribution-platform terms. Rebirth Health does not control the availability or accuracy of those third-party services.

7. Availability and changes

The app is currently an individual-development alpha. Features may change, be unavailable on some devices, or be withdrawn. We may update this agreement when the app or its data practices materially change.

8. Disclaimer

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the app is provided for personal wellness use without a promise that it will be uninterrupted, error-free, or suitable for a particular health purpose. Seek qualified medical help for health concerns or emergencies.
