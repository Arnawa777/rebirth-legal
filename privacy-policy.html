Rebirth Health Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 August 2026

Rebirth Health (Project Rebirth) is a local-first Android wellness and personal-progression application. This policy explains what the app processes, where it is stored, and how optional wearable integrations work.

1. Information processed by the app

Depending on the features you use, the app may process:

  • Persona/profile information that you enter during onboarding.
  • Body measurements and health-baseline values that you enter, such as weight or waist measurements.
  • Journey goals, milestones, reminders, progress records, and related app settings.
  • Optional read-only data from HUAWEI Health when you authorize it, currently limited to steps, calories, and the latest body-weight value.
  • Bluetooth discovery and pairing information when you choose the experimental direct Huawei Watch GT 4 pairing option. Direct watch pairing does not currently synchronize health records.
  • Backup files that you deliberately create or restore using the app.

2. Local-first storage

The current alpha stores app data locally on your Android device. The current app does not operate a developer-owned account system, server, cloud database, advertising system, analytics service, or automatic cloud synchronization. The developer does not receive a remote copy of your local records through the app.

3. Huawei Health and wearable permissions

Huawei Health access is optional. The app requests read access only after you choose to connect and authorize the relevant data types. The app does not write health data back to Huawei Health. Huawei Health, HMS Core, and your device manufacturer may process data under their own terms and privacy policies. You can deny or revoke permissions through the relevant Android or Huawei settings.

4. Notifications and Bluetooth

Local notification permission is used only for reminders you configure. Bluetooth permissions are used only when you choose to discover or pair a Huawei Watch GT 4 through the optional direct-connection experiment.

5. Sharing and disclosure

The current app has no developer-operated backend through which the listed data is routinely shared. You remain responsible for any backup file you export, move, attach, or share with another person or service.

6. Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or replace them through an available app workflow. Backup files remain wherever you save them until you delete them. Because there is no current developer cloud copy, the developer cannot delete data from a remote account.

7. Security

Keep your Android device, Huawei account, and any exported backup files protected. No local-storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Wellness notice

Rebirth Health is for personal wellness and progress tracking. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, emergency monitoring, or treatment recommendations. Do not use it as a substitute for qualified medical advice.

9. Children

The app is intended for individual adult users and is not directed to children.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with material revisions. For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown in the official app listing or the support channel provided with the app distribution.
